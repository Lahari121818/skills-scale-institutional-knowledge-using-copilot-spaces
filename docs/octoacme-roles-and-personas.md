# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX/UI Designers and User Researchers

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers and User Researchers investigate user needs and shape accessible, usable product experiences. Depending on the project, one person may cover both research and design.

### Responsibilities
- Plan and conduct user research, and share findings with the team
- Create user flows, prototypes, and interface designs aligned with product goals
- Validate designs with users and incorporate feedback
- Document usability and accessibility considerations for implementation

### Goals
- Ensure solutions address validated user needs
- Make product experiences clear, accessible, and easy to use
- Reduce design uncertainty before implementation

### Typical Communication
- Research findings and design reviews with Product Managers and Stakeholders
- Design specifications and handoffs with Developers
- Usability and accessibility validation with QA/Testing
- User feedback, design decisions, and open questions in project documentation

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partner with Product Managers to connect user research and design choices to product outcomes and priorities
- Coordinate with Project Managers on research and design milestones, dependencies, and review schedules
- Work with Developers to assess feasibility and clarify design requirements during implementation
- Collaborate with QA/Testing and Stakeholders to validate usability and gather feedback

---

## Tech Leads and Architects

### Role Summary
Tech Leads and Architects guide technical direction and help ensure that solutions are maintainable, secure, and compatible with the project's architecture. The scope may be held by a dedicated lead or shared among Developers.

### Responsibilities
- Guide architecture and implementation decisions, documenting significant trade-offs
- Establish or reinforce engineering standards and technical quality expectations
- Identify technical risks, dependencies, and integration needs
- Support Developers with design reviews and technical problem-solving

### Goals
- Deliver reliable and maintainable technical solutions
- Make technical decisions visible and aligned with product needs
- Reduce technical risks and integration surprises

### Typical Communication
- Technical designs and decision records with Developers
- Estimates, dependencies, and risks with Project Managers
- Feasibility and trade-off discussions with Product Managers
- Technical readiness information for QA/Testing and release partners

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Collaborate with Developers on implementation choices, reviews, and engineering standards
- Advise Product Managers on technical feasibility and the impact of product trade-offs
- Coordinate with Project Managers on dependencies, estimates, technical risks, and milestones
- Work with QA/Testing and Stakeholders to clarify technical acceptance needs and constraints

---

## Release and Operations Leads

### Role Summary
Release and Operations Leads coordinate deployment readiness and operational handoff. This work may be performed by a DevOps or operations specialist, or shared by the delivery team.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate release plans, deployment environments, and operational readiness
- Confirm monitoring, support, and rollback or mitigation plans are prepared
- Identify operational risks and dependencies before release
- Support post-deployment verification and communicate operational outcomes

### Goals
- Make releases predictable and reduce deployment risk
- Ensure services are observable and supportable after release
- Enable timely response to production issues

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness and schedule updates with Project Managers
- Deployment requirements and runbooks with Developers
- Verification plans with QA/Testing
- Operational status and known issues with Stakeholders and customer-facing teams

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Coordinate with Project Managers on release timing, dependencies, risks, and escalation
- Partner with Developers on deployment automation, observability, and operational handoff
- Work with QA/Testing to align staging checks and post-deployment verification
- Inform Product Managers and Stakeholders about operational readiness, issues, and impact

---

## Customer Support and Customer Success Representatives

### Role Summary
Customer Support and Customer Success Representatives bring customer feedback and service-readiness needs into project planning and delivery. Their involvement can vary with the project's customer impact.

### Responsibilities
- Share recurring customer questions, pain points, and product feedback
- Identify documentation, training, and support readiness needs for releases
- Help prepare customer-facing guidance and communicate known issues
- Route post-release feedback and emerging issues to the delivery team

### Goals
- Ensure customer needs inform product decisions
- Prepare support teams and customers for changes
- Close the feedback loop after delivery

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback and priorities with Product Managers
- Readiness, known issues, and escalation paths with Project Managers
- Reproduction details and customer impact with Developers and QA/Testing
- Release guidance and updates with Stakeholders and Release/Operations Leads

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Work with Product Managers to share customer insights and validate that proposed work addresses user needs
- Coordinate with Project Managers on support readiness, communication plans, and customer-impacting risks
- Provide Developers and QA/Testing with customer scenarios and feedback to inform implementation and validation
- Partner with Release/Operations Leads and Stakeholders on launch communications, known issues, and post-release follow-up

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Adapt, combine, or omit roles based on project size, scope, and team needs; the titles do not imply that every project requires a separate person for each role.
